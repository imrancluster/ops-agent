# NNNN. Title as a short decision statement

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who made the call

## Context

What problem or force made us decide something? What constraints apply
(cost, privacy, skills, hardware, time)? Write it so someone who joins a
year from now understands the situation *without asking you*.

## Options considered

1. **Option A:** one line. Pros / cons.
2. **Option B:** one line. Pros / cons.
3. (Doing nothing is often a real option, so list it.)

## Decision

"We will ..." in one or two sentences. Be specific.

## Consequences

- **Good:** what becomes easier.
- **Bad / accepted costs:** what becomes harder, and what we now must do because of it.
- **Revisit when:** the signal that would make us reconsider (e.g. "if we
  need streaming from 3+ providers", "if local pass-rate < 70%").
